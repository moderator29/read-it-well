# The reference film, studied

The founder's reference is a 75-second launch film for a payments-approval product on X, by @granttheeking. The founder sent 33 frames of it on 30 September 2026; they are kept outside the repo. It is for study only. Nothing of its footage, logo, layouts, mascot or music is copied. What we take is how it works.

## Why it feels lovely (the techniques)

1. **The chapter pill.** Every section opens with big words, which then shrink into a small pill at the top centre and stay while the product works under it. With the sound off you always know what you are watching.
2. **Type as objects.**
   - Words land one at a time, at different heights and sizes, with the key word in the accent colour.
   - Words wrap around a device.
   - Hand-drawn underlines draw on.
   - Digits roll like an odometer.
   - Nothing is a static title card.
3. **The real product is the set.** A real browser window (sometimes tilted in 3D) or a real phone, with the camera pushing into the detail that matters. A modal dims the page behind it, which gives depth.
4. **A tactile pointer.** A glossy 3D object stands in for the finger. It travels, squashes on the click, and leaves a few particles. Every click has a result: a panel, a toast, a chip, a count.
5. **Results pile up as cards.** After the key action, the pieces of the result (the request, the log, the rule, the count, the toast) float as separate cards around the device, each with a small motion of its own: a seal stamps, a timeline ticks, a bar grows.
6. **A world of objects.** Glossy objects in the brand colour (coin, card, shield, cloud, check seals, rings, sparkles) float at the frame's edges at different depths. They bob and turn slowly, and the nearest ones are soft-focused. They are always there, never the subject. For Vallo's films these are the platform's own components; the 3D icons are kept for the social campaign.
7. **Rings and particles.** A thin ring draws itself around a key title, with a burst of dots and dashes. Used three or four times, always on a title.
8. **A character.** A small mascot peeks in, waves, and walks along a timeline. It is fun without words.
9. **One contrast beat.** A single dark scene in an otherwise pale film resets the eye before the last act.
10. **Rhythm.** A new chapter every 6–10 s, a small action about every second inside it, and a caption pill at the bottom throughout.
11. **Restraint.** Pale grounds, one accent colour, black type, soft shadows, round corners, lots of air.

## How Vallo uses it, as our own

| Theirs | Ours |
|---|---|
| Lime on pale green, black type | Vallo electric blue (#0069FE, sky #8FD3FF) on mist and white for the product chapters. The night navy of the brand art for the opening, "Going out tonight" and the name at the end. Light and dark are separate chapters, never side by side in one frame. |
| Their glossy coin, cloud and shield | **In the films, the platform's own components**, not decoration: cards, chips, pills, toasts, tiles and buttons lifted from the live screens, floating in depth as the "bodies" around the phone or window. Vallo's 3D clay icons (`apps/web/public/brand/3d/`) appear in the films only where they are part of a real component (the home tiles, an empty state, a card that carries one), and then small. The founder's ruling, 30 September 2026: the 3D icons belong mainly to the social campaign; in the films, very small or not at all unless they are part of the platform. Sparkles, rings and particles are plain shapes. |
| The chapter pill with their mark | A pill with the Vallo mark and a short phrase from FACTS, one word in blue: "The full cost, **up front**", "Talk to the **owner**", "Book a **room**". |
| The jelly-bean pointer | Desktop: a glossy blue orb pointer that dips on each click, with a ring. Mobile: the tap ripple under the glass. |
| The mascot | None invented. The product's own 3D assistant robot appears only in the assistant chapter, peeking and nodding. |
| "Approve from anywhere" around a phone | "Talk to the **owner**", "Book a **room**" and "Speaks your **language**", wrapped around the 3D phone. |
| The PIN keypad | Vallo's real passcode: money screens sit behind it (docs/PASSCODE.md), so "when it's time to pay" shows the passcode filling, honestly. |
| Result cards around the device | After a booking, a reservation or a payment: the pieces as cards around the phone (the dates, "3 nights", the resort, "Room booked · Example"), each with its own small motion. |
| The pill wall | The contrast beat: a night wall of pills with Nigerian cities and places, lighting up as guests come "from across the country". |
| Squiggle underlines, odometer digits, rings with particles | Used as they are: the move-in total rolls into ₦26,100,000; "done right" gets its underline; the ring draws around "Meet Vallo". |
| End card with one CTA pill | The Vallo mark and "Coming soon on iPhone and Android" (or the official badges once live), plus vallospaces.com. |

The rules in FACTS.md still hold. Every number, name and claim is Vallo's own, illustrative cards carry the Example chip, and nothing implies Vallo holds money.
