Worth knowing before you start
- Message whoever listed a place from inside Vallo, so the conversation
stays on the record. Places marked Example are there to show how Vallo
works and cannot be rented or booked.
- Vallo has two sides on one account. Property is renting, buying and
selling; Vallo Stays is hotels, apartments, guest houses, resorts and
restaurant tables.
- On a tenancy the rent is rarely the whole number. Caution deposit,
agency, legal, agreement and service charge are normal here, so the
move-in total is printed in full before you commit.
- Keep chats and payments inside Vallo. Inspect a property before you
pay for it, and pay for a stay at checkout rather than into anybody's
account.
