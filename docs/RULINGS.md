# Founder rulings D51 to D85: what is still in force

Code comments, migrations and probes cite the founder's rulings by number
(`D51`, `D68d`, `D83` and so on). They were recorded in
`docs/sessions/DIRECTIVES-2026-10-05.md`, a 200 KB session log that is now
deleted (recover it with `git show cfb3e974b^:docs/sessions/DIRECTIVES-2026-10-05.md`).
This file is the replacement: one line per ruling, checked against the code on
17 October 2026. Where a ruling and this file disagree with the code, the code
wins and this file is fixed.

Precedence: `HANDOFF.md` section 2, then `DECISIONS_2026-10-13.md`, then this
file. D71 was never written (the number was skipped). D77, D79, D84 and D85
were never written up as prose; the lines below come from the migrations and
code comments that carry them.

## In force

- **D51** (6 October). Every rate is policy data, never a constant: Vallo's commission is a `fee_rates` row read through `private.current_fee_bps('commission')` (set at 2 percent, live rows may differ; the Terms still say zero, an open conflict for the solicitor). The Vallo Guarantee is retired: `money_policy.guarantee_bps` is 0 live, the machinery (`guarantee_reserve_entries`, claims) stays built and the money sentences never mention it (`lib/money/copy.ts`). The referral engine is paid from three separate pots. D52 corrected D51's mechanism (no `commission_bps` column in `money_policy`; the Guarantee check constraint needed a migration, not a row): read D51 with D52.
- **D58** (6 October). The referral admin centre at `/admin/referrals`: attribution, qualification, detection and payouts, with the detectors specified in `docs/referral/REFERRAL_ADMIN_CENTRE.md`. Superseded on engine, lifecycle and configuration by D62.
- **D60** (6 October). Paid promotion exists and is a product (`lib/promotion`, `docs/promotion/VALLO_PROMOTION.md`): the no-paid-placement doctrine in older documents is dead. Tiers are Boost, Spotlight and Featured (`lib/promotion/tiers.ts`).
- **D61** (6 October). The fee acceptance screen quotes the rail table, not a flat percentage: escrow rail is Vallo's commission plus the partner's escrow fee, direct rail is Vallo's commission plus a capped processor fee. `lister_fee_policy` and `lister_fee_accept` record what the lister accepted (`lib/money/lister-fee.ts`).
- **D62** (6 October). The referral architecture is `docs/referral/REFERRAL_ARCHITECTURE.md`: Vallo rewards genuine network growth, not account creation. No reward amount, threshold, window or cap is a product rule; all are campaign configuration rows. D62's hold on the migration was lifted by D63.
- **D63** (6 October). The referral migration is applied (`20261006152509_b4_referral_rewards_engine`). Phone verification is built with Termii and was never to be switched off; a referral counts only after the invited member verifies a phone (`fx01_referral_counts_after_phone_verified`).
- **D64** (6 October). The platform-wide referral budget is 700,000 naira a month, one row, checked server-side before a reward accrues; an exhausted budget pauses rewards, it never refuses them, and a paused reward resumes when the next month's budget opens (D66 and D67 settled how). The per-member cap is 1,500 qualified referrals a month.
- **D68c** (7 October). The app opening is the product arriving, never a logo: nothing paints the logo by itself on app open, the native launch image is a plain `#010118` field, and the gate is decided before first paint (`components/startup`).
- **D68d** (7 October). The rail decides the gate (`20261007154216`): on the escrow rail the partner holds the money so no staff approval is needed before payment; the staff review gate exists only for the direct rail, driven by risk signals (see D83 ruling 1 for the switch).
- **D69** (7 October). ADR 0003: a licensed provider holds the money, Vallo records it. Escrow, balances, deposits and withdrawals exist as Vallo-native screens; Payluk holds the funds; the custody naming guard (`private.refuse_custody_objects`) stays. `docs/adr/0003-a-licensed-provider-holds-the-money-vallo-records-it.md`.
- **D70** (7 October). Every money page has a door: Payments, Receipts, Payouts, Refunds, Rewards, Invite friends and the leaderboards sit in a Money group in the side navigation (`lib/nav/route-parents.ts`).
- **D72** (7 October). The whole platform is built to the premium standard in `docs/design/PREMIUM-STANDARD.md`, on web, Android and iOS. Get Started is the onboarding card set ("Find your space.", 1 of 4). The "six agents" process part is replaced by `HANDOFF.md` section 5 (at most four agents).
- **D73** (7 October). Two ways to pay: negotiated property deals (rent, sale) go through the agreement and then escrow held by the licensed partner; fixed-price bookings (hotels, shortlets, restaurants) are paid by card directly. Refined on 13 October (`DECISIONS_2026-10-13.md` rows 21 and 22): rent and shortlets let the payer pick Escrow (default) or card, a sale is always escrow, a hotel room is always card. Escrow shows only while `protectedRailLive()` reads true.
- **D74** (7 October). The Plasma frames are the level of premium, platform wide; a platinum material joined the token set and dark stays the default. D78 narrowed it: platinum never sits on a control.
- **D75** (7 October). Bookings end to end in Vallo's own dialect of the best booking platforms: shortlet search with dates and guests, the listing page with gallery, host, rules, policy and reviews, the sticky price bar, the host calendar, hotel rooms and rates, restaurant tables with confirm, seated and no-show. Restaurant card deposits are built behind `restaurant_deposits`, which is off and stays off while table booking is free (`HANDOFF.md` known conflicts).
- **D76** (7 October). Two public leaderboards, City and Global: referrals and top performers by completed deals and bookings, never by naira; opt-out; only verified workspaces and real activity; a who-to-follow strip in the feed; one agents directory for both sides (`app/(app)/leaderboard`, `components/app/leaderboard`). Its wallet two-action bar was replaced by D80 and then D81.
- **D77** (7 October). The gate rulings (`20261007154344_d77_founder_rulings_on_the_gate`): the first-deal signal fires only on a first deal AND (an unverified business OR an amount over the threshold); the direct-rail review threshold is 500,000 naira and stays configuration; a sale never routes to the direct rail; the lister's fee acceptance is recorded; one Payluk switch, `payments_payluk_on`, read by the app and by `private.rentals_protected_pay_on`; staff risk settings at `/admin/agreements/settings`; opening and funding an escrow payment ask the gate.
- **D78** (7 October, evening). Blue is the identity and there is no glass: no platinum and no white on controls, every call to action is the primary blue button, containers are the blue-tinted container, the passcode keypad is blue, and icons are solid opaque blue renders everywhere (including the dock at rest, `HANDOFF.md` section 2). Gold is untouched by this ruling.
- **D79** (7 October). Social surfaces use bare glyphs: the profile's Back is a glyph on the cover with no plate and no word, action pills are one bare glyph, the compact rung is smaller and clean, the profile menu opens wholly on screen, and a post card opens the post through the router rather than an overlay.
- **D80** (8 October). The feed takes its own next-generation look, researched away from X, with bare action icons (D78). Its wallet design was replaced by D81.
- **D81** (8 October). The new logo (`docs/design/references/2026-10-08/logo-mark-new.png` and the wordmark) replaces every old mark everywhere; orange is used in more places as the second accent (blue leads, orange sparks) and the Send capsule is the one orange fill; the Wallet follows `GOVERNING-wallet-overview-transactions.png` (Overview and Transactions tabs, the balance card, filter chips, rows, a More options group, exactly two persistent bottom capsules); Get Started opens on Sign in or Sign up unless seen in this sitting (30 minutes).
- **D82** (8 October). The logo and wordmark have one colour set on every ground, light or dark: no per-theme swap, no plate, no filter. No glass logo anywhere: app icons on every platform, notification icon, welcome coin, PWA screenshots, emails, share cards, splash, inner pages.
- **D83** (8 October). Five settled rulings: (1) no staff approval before payment, behind `agreement_staff_review` written as a row and OFF (the second confirmation of the same terms approves the agreement); (2) the first-deal signal is scoped as in D77; (3) the direct-rail threshold is 500,000 naira, revisit after 100 real transactions; (4) the lister pays the partner's escrow fee on both rails; (5) Vallo Pro at 9,500 naira a month and Vallo Business at 35,000 a month, above the free plan, each bundling promotion worth more than its price, with a 4 day trial held as configuration. Also: `aps-environment` stays `development` in the entitlements file; `APNS_PRODUCTION=true` is set on the production target only.
- **D84** (8 October). The database side of D83 (`20261008171128_d84_founder_rulings_8_october`, probe `supabase/tests/probes/d84-founder-rulings.sql`): the switch row, the confirm function and the two paid plan rows.
- **D85** (8 October). Invite and Earn: referral sign-up through `/join`, the invite carried in sign-up metadata or claimed afterwards for a new account only (`users_referral_attribute`), the earnings card and the one plain line under each referred person, Withdraw always present on the rewards screen, the qualify, release and flag-clusters cron jobs (`PG_CRON_JOBS`), and money sentences built from the campaign's own requirement keys, never a hard-coded figure.
