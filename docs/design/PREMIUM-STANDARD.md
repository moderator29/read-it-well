# The premium standard (7 October 2026, D72)

The founder's instruction on 7 October, after the first round of this session: upgrade
the whole platform, every page, to a standard somebody would pay two million dollars
for. Clean, consistent, next generation, with motion that makes people stop. Android,
iOS and web. This file is the bar every agent builds to. It sits on top of
`ONE-PRODUCT-DECISIONS.md` (how each thing is done, once) and the existing doctrine
(`CRAFT_DOCTRINE.md`, `MOTION_SYSTEM.md`, `GLOW_IDENTITY.md`, `VISUAL_NORTH_STAR_2026-10-05.md`).

Two rulings stay above everything: **dark is the default**, and **the container system
is refined, not replaced.**

## The bar, per screen

Before calling a screen done, ask it in his words: is this industry standard, would a
person pay two million dollars for this, would he love it. Then check:

1. **One thing.** The screen is for one thing and that thing is the biggest element on
   it.
2. **Breathing room.** Generous space above titles and between groups. Crowding is the
   cheapest look there is.
3. **Four type sizes at most.** Display or figure, title, body, caption. Figures in
   `--nf-font-numeric`, tabular.
4. **Three elevations.** Base, raised, floating glass. Glass only for what floats.
5. **One radius per role**, from tokens.
6. **Icons:** thin, consistent outline glyphs from `UiIcon` at small sizes for controls
   (the feed action row, list rows). The 3D objects (`public/brand/tier-a/`, and the
   finance set) are **illustration**, used where a screen needs to explain an idea
   (Get Started, empty states, success moments, the flip), never as decoration on
   every row.
7. **Motion:** framer-motion (installed, `^12`) for orchestrated sequences and shared
   element transitions; CSS for the rest. Entrances rise and settle on `land`, exits
   on `leave`, stagger at most 60ms. Shared elements carry across screens (a card
   becomes the page it opens; a figure becomes the receipt figure). Every motion has a
   reduced-motion, Calm and Off fallback. Five things move beautifully; everything
   else is still.
8. **The four states designed:** empty (with the next step and, where it helps, a 3D
   object), loading (a skeleton of the real structure), error (says what to do), and
   success (a payoff moment).
9. **Tap targets 44px or more**, on Android and iOS and the web, and nothing scrolls
   sideways at 320px.
10. **Never fabricate.** No invented balance, count, review or listing. A beautiful
    empty state beats a fake full one.
