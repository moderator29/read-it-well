## How to read this

**NOT WALKED means nobody looked.** It is not a pass and it is not a failure.
Every one carries the reason it could not be reached, and most of those reasons
are the signed-out gate in `src/proxy.ts`: this machine has no way to hold a
real session, so a route behind that gate cannot be opened here at all.

**`control asked for` is not `where it landed`.** A control that pushes the right
parent and is then bounced by the gate is CORRECT, and the note says so. A wrong
destination and a correct destination behind a gate look identical from the
address bar and have different owners.

**The Android column is what `isAppRoot` answers.** A ROOT exits the shell; a
declared route goes to its parent; an undeclared route goes to a fallback and
never exits, which is the safe direction by design.
