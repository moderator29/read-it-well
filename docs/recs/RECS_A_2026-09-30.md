# Recommendations A: the front door (30 September 2026)

Auditor A's area is the landing page and the public site, sign in, sign up and
`/welcome`, emails and push, share images, SEO and metadata, the speed of
public pages, and trust and conversion.

These are recommendations only. Nothing here has been built.

**Already done or planned, so not repeated here:**

- UIUX items 6 to 12, 23, 24 and 25 (`docs/UIUX_30_RECOMMENDATIONS_2026-09-29.md`).
- The perf sweep and its open items (`docs/PERF_SWEEP_2026-09-29.md`).
- The lock-screen copy rules (`docs/EMAIL_NOTIFICATION_COPY_2026-09-29.md`).
- From the sibling files: calendar files and viewing reminders (B5),
  translating the 21 English-only namespaces (C11), passkey unlock for the
  passcode (C14), and accessibility tests for the desks (C16).

**How the evidence was gathered.**

- Code reading on branch `claude/vallo-production-audit-a2dgqs`.
- A locale coverage count run through `suppliedKeys` from `@vallo/i18n`.
- Playwright against the shared dev server on 30 September, at 390x844
  (light and dark) and 1440x900, on `/`, `/sign-up/email`, `/sign-in`,
  `/welcome`, `/check` and `/help`.

The dev server's timings are compile timings, so they are not quoted as
performance.

## Conversion: the path from stranger to member

### A1. Sign up on one screen; ask the rest after the first result

- **Today.**
  - Item 8 closed the catalogue, so every stranger has to make an account
    before they see a single listing. The sign-up form is therefore the most
    important form Vallo has.
  - `/sign-up/email` is two steps with 10 inputs (measured). Step one asks for
    first name, surname, email, password and confirm password. Step two asks
    for nickname, state and LGA, "where did you hear about us" (required,
    `lib/auth/actions.ts` line 164), a referral code, the 18+ tick and the
    terms (`components/auth/EmailAuthForm.tsx`).
  - The hero above the form says "New here? Your account takes a minute."
    The form does not bear that out.
  - The door from the landing takes five screens before the form's last field:
    Get started, then `/start`, then the `/welcome` intro, then `/sign-up`
    options, then step one, then step two. The verify code comes after that.
- **Proposal.**
  - Sign-up keeps only these: email, one password field with show and hide
    (the eye is already there), first name, and the terms and 18+ line as one
    sentence under the button ("By creating an account you confirm you are 18
    or over and agree to the Terms").
  - Drop "confirm password". Show and hide plus the reset flow cover the typo
    it guards against.
  - Move state and LGA, nickname, surname and occupation into progressive
    asks. Each is asked once, at the moment it is needed:
    - state and LGA: the first search, used as the default area;
    - surname: the first enquiry or booking, where a legal name is needed;
    - hear-about: a one-tap optional chip row on the first `/home`.
  - The Get started door goes straight to the sign-up form with the social
    doors on top. The `/welcome` intro becomes the screen after
    verification, not the one before the form.
- **Impact.** Sign-up completion is the platform's main conversion number while
  the catalogue is gated. Going from 10 inputs to 4 is the largest single lever
  available on the front door.
- **Effort** M. **Priority** P1.
- **Founder decision.** Hear-about becomes optional and is asked later (it is
  required today). Surname moves to the first transaction.

### A2. Sign in with a phone number, the code sent by WhatsApp

- **Today.**
  - Sign in and sign up are email, Google (web only) or Apple
    (`app/(auth)/sign-in/page.tsx`).
  - Many Nigerian renters live in WhatsApp and a phone number, not an inbox.
  - `lib/phone-otp/` is built (core, gate, send, transport) but sits behind
    `feature_flags.phone_confirmation`, which is off until "a code transport is
    wired (founder question 7)" (`lib/phone-otp/flag.ts`).
  - `lib/notify/whatsapp.ts` already has Meta Cloud API plumbing. Its policy
    allows "SMS only for one-time codes".
- **Proposal.**
  - Use Meta's Authentication template category (a one-time code with a
    copy-code button) as the transport, with SMS as the fallback when the
    number is not on WhatsApp.
  - Add "Continue with phone number" as the first door on `/sign-up` and
    `/sign-in`.
  - The field has a fixed +234 prefix. It takes 080... or 80... and
    normalises through `lib/phone.ts`.
  - The code field reuses `VerifyCodeForm`, which already has
    `one-time-code` autofill and auto-submits at 6 digits.
  - An email is asked for later, only when a receipt or agreement needs one.
- **Impact.** Removes the inbox step for the people least likely to check one.
  It also gives the agent check at `/check` and the WhatsApp doorbell a
  confirmed number to match against.
- **Effort** L. **Priority** P2.
- **Founder decision.** Question 7: the transport (WhatsApp authentication
  templates, an SMS aggregator or both) and the per-code cost ceiling. Also
  whether a phone-only account may transact before it adds an email.

### A3. Sign in without a password: an email code and passkeys

- **Today.**
  - Sign in is email plus password (`EmailAuthForm.tsx`, "Forgot password?" on
    the right).
  - WebAuthn is already built for the money lock and the console
    (`lib/security/webauthn.ts`, `webauthn-client.ts`, `MoneyStepUp.tsx`), but
    it is never offered as a way to sign in.
  - C14 covers unlocking the passcode, not signing in.
- **Proposal.**
  - On `/sign-in`, after the email: "Email me a code instead" as a quiet
    secondary. It uses Supabase `signInWithOtp` with the same 6-digit
    template the verify step uses.
  - On a device that holds a Vallo passkey, the browser's passkey autofill
    (`autocomplete="username webauthn"` on the email field) signs the person
    in with one touch.
  - After a successful sign-in on a device without a passkey: "Sign in faster
    next time with your fingerprint or face", shown once, and easy to dismiss.
- **Impact.** Forgotten passwords are the most common way people lose a
  returning session on shared or new phones. Passkeys also resist the phishing
  pages that copy property sites.
- **Effort** M. **Priority** P2.
- **Founder decision.** None, beyond agreeing that passkeys may sign in and
  not only step up.

### A4. The code screen that helps when the code does not come

- **Today.**
  - `VerifyCodeForm.tsx` has a ghost "resend" button with no countdown. It
    gives no hint about Spam or Promotions (Gmail files many first mails
    there), no quick way to open the mail app, and no easy way to fix a
    mistyped address beyond the email field.
  - A person who never gets the code is lost at the last step of the funnel.
- **Proposal.**
  - A 30-second countdown on "Send a new code". After two sends it adds the
    line "Still nothing? Check Spam and Promotions for mail from Vallo".
  - "Open Gmail" and "Open Outlook" buttons when the domain matches (an
    `https://mail.google.com/mail/u/0/#search/from:vallo` link, and the app
    scheme inside the native shell).
  - The address shown in bold, with "Wrong address? Change it" returning to
    step one with everything else kept.
  - Resends are logged (A6) so the drop-off is visible.
- **Impact.** Recovers people who have already done all the work.
- **Effort** S. **Priority** P1.
- **Founder decision.** None.

### A5. The referral code field: give people a code to share, or remove it

- **Today.**
  - Sign-up step two asks for a "Referral code" (`en.ts` line 1450). It is
    checked against a pattern and stored in the account metadata
    (`lib/auth/actions.ts` line 424).
  - No screen anywhere gives a member a code or an invite link. `grep` for
    "invite a friend", "referral link" and "your referral" finds nothing.
  - So the field asks a question nobody can answer.
- **Proposal.** Choose one.
  - **(a) Build it.** `/join/[code]` pre-fills the code, carries it through
    `?next`, and has its own share card: "{First name} invited you to Vallo",
    with no incentive claims. Each member gets a "Share Vallo" row in
    Settings with the link and WhatsApp share. The admin console gets a count
    of verified sign-ups by code.
  - **(b) Remove it.** Take the field out of sign-up (A1 would do this
    anyway) until (a) exists.
- **Impact.** Agents and hosts already bring their own renters and guests.
  Recording who they bring is the cheapest growth loop the platform has, and
  it is honest if no reward is promised.
- **Effort** M for (a), S for (b). **Priority** P2.
- **Founder decision.** (a) or (b), and whether any reward exists. A reward
  would need terms and a claims-lint entry.

### A6. A first-party funnel you can read: from landing to first result

- **Today.**
  - There is no product analytics. `package.json` has no PostHog, Plausible or
    Vercel Analytics, and `lib/observability` holds web vitals, sampled at 1
    in 10 with 2 rows landed (perf sweep).
  - The founder cannot tell how many strangers reach sign-up, where they drop
    off, or whether A1 to A4 work.
- **Proposal.**
  - A small `funnel_events` table written by a server action and by the auth
    callbacks. No third-party script and no cookie beyond the session.
  - Ten named steps:
    1. landing view
    2. Get started
    3. sign-up opened
    4. step one done
    5. step two done
    6. code sent
    7. code resent
    8. verified
    9. first search
    10. first save or enquiry
  - Each event carries the locale, the surface, the door
    (hero/search/category/footer) and a random visit id. There is no user id
    before verification.
  - An admin "Front door" card shows the step-to-step conversion over 7 and 30
    days, split by locale and surface.
  - Retention of 90 days, added to `docs/RETENTION_SCHEDULE.md` and the privacy
    page.
- **Impact.** Every other item in this file becomes measurable. It also shows
  whether the Hausa, Yoruba and Igbo front doors convert (A10).
- **Effort** M. **Priority** P1.
- **Founder decision.** First-party only (recommended). The retention period
  and the privacy-page wording, since this adds a processing purpose under the
  NDPA.

## Trust you can check

### A7. "Check before you pay" as a front-door feature, not a hidden page

- **Today.**
  - `/check` ("Is this a Vallo agent?", V-61) and `/r` (the receipt check) are
    the most distinctive trust tools on the platform, and both are open to
    strangers.
  - The agents' own copy tells them to print "vallospaces.com/check"
    (`trust-doors.en.ts` line 51).
  - Yet neither page is linked from the landing, the header or the footer
    (`SiteFooter.tsx` lists Buy, Rent, Stays, About, Careers, Help, Docs,
    Safety, Standards and Cancellations). Neither is in `PUBLIC_PAGES`
    (`lib/listings/sitemap.ts`), and both share the site-wide generic
    `opengraph-image.jpg` (measured).
- **Proposal.**
  - A landing room (or a band in the Community room) called "Check before you
    pay". It holds the `/check` field inline: paste a number or a VA- code and
    get the answer in place. It links to "Got a Vallo receipt? Check it".
  - "Check an agent" in the header's More menu and in the footer's Support
    column.
  - Add `/check` and `/r` to `PUBLIC_PAGES`.
  - A dedicated share card for `/check`, with no query in it.
  - After a "no match" result, a "Share this warning" button that sends a
    fixed WhatsApp text: "This number is not a registered Vallo agent. Check
    any agent at vallospaces.com/check". It never includes the number itself.
- **Impact.** It turns Vallo's anti-scam promise into something a stranger can
  use in ten seconds without an account. That is the strongest reason to
  trust the brand before signing up, and a reason to come back.
- **Effort** S. **Priority** P1.
- **Founder decision.** Whether the warning share may be sent from a no-match
  result (recommended, since it names no one).

### A8. The move-in total in the first screen, as a calculator anyone can use

- **Today.**
  - The whole promise is "the real cost up front" (hero sub-line, fact row,
    welcome chip "Your move-in total, printed").
  - At 390 the first screen is text, a button and a search form (screenshot
    `land-390-light`). The move-in total itself is only visible inside a
    gated listing (`ListingMoveInBlock.tsx`).
  - No public calculator exists (no "calculator" hits outside listing and pay
    code).
- **Proposal.**
  - A public `/move-in-cost` tool, plus a compact version of it in the hero at
    1440 (right column) and under the fact row on phones.
  - The visitor enters the yearly rent and picks a state. The tool prints the
    lines Vallo already models: rent, agency, legal, caution and service
    charge, where each applies. It shows them as the summary card and bar
    from the listing (UIUX 18).
  - Every percentage has a label with its source ("typical in Lagos, set by
    the lister on Vallo"). The total is marked "An estimate. Each listing on
    Vallo shows its own."
  - "Save this" and "Share as image" use the share-card frame. The call to
    action is "See listings with the total already worked out", which leads
    to sign-up.
- **Impact.** It shows the product's core value before the sign-up wall. It is
  a natural thing to share on WhatsApp ("this is what N1.5m really costs"). It
  is also an indexable page for high-intent searches.
- **Effort** M. **Priority** P2.
- **Founder decision.** Which fee conventions may be printed as typical, and
  from what source. The claims lint needs each one backed, or the tool shows
  empty fields for the visitor to fill in.

### A9. A front door for supply: public pages for hosts, agents and landlords

- **Today.**
  - "Become an agent" in the footer and on `/about` goes to `/agents`. Since
    the old funnel was retired, `next.config.ts` line 169 redirects that to
    `/profile?switch=owner`, which is gated.
  - So a prospective agent, host or landlord clicks and lands on a sign-in
    wall with no word about what listing costs, what is checked, or how and
    when payouts arrive.
  - There is no public host or landlord page at all.
- **Proposal.** Three short public pages: `/for-agents`, `/for-hosts` and
  `/for-landlords`. Each has:
  - the steps (register, verify: the documents named from the verification
    rungs, list, get enquiries or bookings, get paid);
  - a fee table rendered from the money constants
    (`docs/MONEY_ARCHITECTURE.md`), so it cannot drift;
  - payout timing as the code defines it;
  - "What Vallo checks and what it does not";
  - one real example screen of the desk, labelled Example.

  The footer link points to the right page. "Start" on each page goes to
  sign-up with `?next=/profile/setup/owner`.
- **Impact.** On a marketplace, supply comes first. Today the platform has no
  way to explain itself to the people who would fill it.
- **Effort** M. **Priority** P1.
- **Founder decision.** The fee wording that may be stated publicly, and
  whether landlords (V-landlord flows) get their own page now.

## Reach: languages, search and sharing

### A10. Languages you can link to: `/ha`, `/yo`, `/ig` for the public site

- **Today.**
  - The language comes from a cookie or `Accept-Language` (`lib/locale.ts`).
    The URL never changes, so a Yoruba landing page cannot be shared, and
    search engines only ever see English.
  - The root metadata is hard-coded English (`app/layout.tsx`: title,
    description, `openGraph.locale: "en_NG"`), and so are the auth titles
    ("Sign in", "Create your account").
  - Measured coverage of what a stranger meets, in `ha`, `yo` and `ig` alike:

    | Namespace | Translated |
    |---|---|
    | `landingRooms` | 0 of 163 |
    | `authFlow` (sign-in notices) | 0 of 52 |
    | `trustDoors` (`/check`, `/r`) | 0 of 80 |
    | `shape` (the wall headings) | 0 of 326 |

  - Result: a Hausa reader gets the hero in Hausa and every room below it in
    English.
  - The sign-up server errors are 20 English literals in
    `lib/auth/actions.ts`.
- **Proposal.**
  - Locale subpaths for the landing, `(site)` and `(auth)` only, through the
    proxy. `/` stays English, and the cookie choice redirects to the matching
    path.
  - `alternates.languages` hreflang on every public page, `og:locale` per
    language, and localised `title` and `description` through
    `generateMetadata`.
  - The language switch keeps the path.
  - Server validation returns keys, not sentences.
  - Within the speaker programme in C11, put the front-door namespaces first:
    `landingRooms`, `authFlow`, `trustDoors`, then `shape`. Add a CI gate
    that fails when a public or auth page renders an English fallback in
    another locale.
- **Impact.** The three languages become reachable from search, WhatsApp and
  radio or flyer campaigns. That is a real edge no competitor has. It also
  makes the static pages in A15 possible, because the locale no longer needs
  a cookie.
- **Effort** L. **Priority** P1.
- **Founder decision.** Subpaths (recommended) or subdomains. Whether
  machine-drafted copy may ship once a speaker has reviewed it.

### A11. Emails, auth mail and push in the member's own language

- **Today.**
  - Not one email builder takes a locale: 0 references in
    `lib/email/messages.ts`, `render.ts`, `welcome-message.ts` and
    `lib/push/copy.ts`.
  - The Supabase auth templates (`supabase/templates/*.html`) are English
    only.
  - So a member who uses Vallo in Igbo gets every booking, payment and
    security message in English.
  - C11 covers the in-app strings, not outbound messages.
- **Proposal.**
  - Store `profiles.locale` whenever the member changes language.
  - Every builder takes a `Dictionary`. The existing length limits (subject
    45, push title 40) are tested per locale through `fixtures.ts`.
  - Auth mail: Supabase templates cannot branch well on locale, so send the
    verification and reset codes through `lib/email` (which already builds
    `verificationCode` and `passwordReset`) using a Supabase Send Email hook.
  - Security mail (new device, password changed) always adds a one-line
    English summary under the translated body, so anyone helping can read it.
- **Impact.** The moment that matters most (money moved, booking confirmed)
  arrives in the language the person chose.
- **Effort** L. **Priority** P2.
- **Founder decision.** Whether to move auth mail off the Supabase templates
  and onto the Send Email hook.

### A12. One-click unsubscribe and a preferences page with no sign-in

- **Today.**
  - `listUnsubscribeHeaders` in `lib/email/client.ts` points
    `List-Unsubscribe` at `/settings/notifications`, which needs a sign-in.
    Its own comment says it is "Not one-click (no List-Unsubscribe-Post):
    that needs a signed, sign-in-free endpoint, which does not exist yet".
  - Gmail and Yahoo require RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe from bulk senders.
    Without it, the Unsubscribe link in Gmail becomes a spam report, and that
    hurts delivery of the verification codes (A4) on the same domain.
- **Proposal.**
  - `/api/email/unsubscribe` takes a signed token (HMAC of user id, channel
    and expiry) and answers POST with `List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` by
    switching that channel off.
  - A matching `/email/preferences?token=` page shows the member's channels
    as switches, with no sign-in. It shows the email masked and never shows
    account data.
  - Transactional and security mail stays outside it, as today.
- **Impact.** Protects deliverability of the codes the whole funnel depends on,
  meets the mailbox providers' rules, and respects people's choices.
- **Effort** S. **Priority** P1.
- **Founder decision.** None.

### A13. Structured data and a share card for every public page

- **Today.**
  - Every public page shares one `og:image` (`app/opengraph-image.jpg`,
    measured on `/`, `/check`, `/help`, `/welcome` and the auth pages). Only
    area pages, price areas and share doors draw their own.
  - The only JSON-LD on the site is `FAQPage` on the landing. `/help` has
    none, although it is a question-and-answer page (measured).
  - There is no `Organization` or `WebSite` block. Google therefore has no
    stated name, logo or legal entity to show, and "VALLO SPACES LTD" is
    printed in the footer but never declared.
- **Proposal.**
  - `Organization` JSON-LD on the root layout: the legal name, the logo, the
    support contact from `lib/support-email.ts`, and `sameAs` only for
    accounts that exist.
  - `WebSite`, with `SearchAction` only when `publicCatalogueEnabled()` is
    true.
  - `FAQPage` on `/help` from its existing items.
  - `BreadcrumbList` on `/docs/[slug]`.
  - One `opengraph-image.tsx` per public page family (help, safety,
    standards, check, about, for-* from A9) drawn with the ShareCardFrame
    from UIUX 23, in the page's locale (A10).
- **Impact.** Search results with a brand panel and a logo. WhatsApp and
  X unfurls that say what the page is, instead of a generic poster.
- **Effort** S. **Priority** P2.
- **Founder decision.** Which social accounts are real, for `sameAs`.

### A14. Guides that rank while the catalogue is closed

- **Today.**
  - `sitemap.ts` names 13 static pages and the area pages. There are no area
    pages yet, because each needs the Price Check minimum of real listings
    (V-82).
  - With inventory gated and the area pages empty, Vallo has almost nothing
    for Nigerian renters' most common searches ("caution fee meaning",
    "agency fee Lagos", "how to know a fake agent", "shortlet scam").
  - Its help and docs pages are about the product, not the renter's problem.
- **Proposal.**
  - A `/guides` section of 10 to 12 evergreen pages, each 600 to 1,000 words,
    in all four languages (A10). Examples:
    - checking an agent before paying (it ends at `/check`);
    - what each move-in line means (it ends at A8);
    - viewing safely;
    - shortlet red flags;
    - what a tenancy agreement should contain;
    - what the Lagos tenancy law says about advance rent (legal review).
  - Every claim is cited, and every guide has a "Last reviewed" date.
  - Each has `Article` JSON-LD and its own share card, and is added to
    `PUBLIC_PAGES`.
- **Impact.** Brings in people who are searching for help right now, which is
  the best moment to be trusted, without opening the catalogue.
- **Effort** L. **Priority** P2.
- **Founder decision.** Who writes and who reviews (legal for the law guides),
  and the refresh schedule.

## Speed, access and polish

### A15. Serve the public pages from the CDN, with a speed budget in CI

- **Today.**
  - Every page is dynamic because the CSP nonce reads `headers()` (perf sweep
    open item 1), and `getLocale()` reads cookies.
  - The landing page's inline scripts are fixed strings: `INTRO_SCRIPT` in
    `app/(landing)/page.tsx`, the theme and side scripts in
    `app/layout.tsx`, and the FAQ JSON-LD. Their hashes never change.
  - No build check limits the size of the public pages.
  - The landing is still 9,628px at 390 and 8,111px at 1440 (measured).
    UIUX item 9 targeted under 8,000 and under 6,500.
- **Proposal.**
  - For the landing, `(site)` and `(auth)`: a CSP with `'sha256-...'` hashes
    for those fixed scripts in place of the nonce.
  - Take the locale from the path (A10), so the pages can use
    `revalidate = 300`.
  - A CI job runs Lighthouse on `/`, `/sign-up/email`, `/check` and one
    guide, on a slow 4G mid-range phone profile. Budgets: LCP 2.5 s, JS under
    170 KB gzipped for the first load, CLS 0.05. It also fails when landing
    height passes the UIUX 9 targets.
- **Impact.** The first byte on `/` comes at CDN speed for every visitor in
  Nigeria, and the budget keeps later passes from undoing it.
- **Effort** M. **Priority** P2.
- **Founder decision.** The owner of the CSP accepts hashes for these three
  route groups.

### A16. An accessibility gate on the front door, in four languages

- **Today.**
  - No accessibility engine is in the repo (`@axe-core` is absent from
    `package.json`).
  - The front door has the most varied surfaces on the platform: the night
    hero islands inside light pages, glass objects, the deck in the "Hands
    on" room, the segmented control in the search pill, and the two-step
    form.
  - C16 covers the desks only.
- **Proposal.**
  - `@axe-core/playwright` over `/`, `/sign-in`, `/sign-up/email` (both
    steps), `/welcome`, `/check`, `/help` and `/safety`, at 390 and 1440, in
    light, dark and Calm motion, in all four locales. Serious or critical
    findings fail CI.
  - Checks axe cannot make, scripted:
    - the page reflows at 200% zoom with no horizontal scroll;
    - the "Hands on" deck works by keyboard and screen reader (one control
      per card, the position announced as "2 of 4");
    - the segmented control is a radio group with arrow keys;
    - each form step's errors are announced (`aria-live`) and move focus to
      the first bad field;
    - the `lang` attribute is correct on locale pages (A10).
- **Impact.** Keeps the redesign usable for low-vision, keyboard and
  screen-reader visitors, the first people to leave a front door that shuts
  them out.
- **Effort** S. **Priority** P2.
- **Founder decision.** None.

### A17. The moment after "verified": arrive somewhere real, with a small celebration

- **Today.**
  - After the code is accepted, the person goes through `/welcome` again,
    this time as a member (`FirstRun` with interests), then to `/home`. A
    stranger who typed "Yaba" into the hero search and was sent through the
    sign-up wall has to look for Yaba again.
  - The `/start?next=` carry (UIUX 12) keeps the destination, but nothing
    marks the arrival.
  - The search typed on the landing (`SearchPill`), and the market chosen, are
    not part of what is carried.
- **Proposal.**
  - Carry the typed query and the Buy, Rent or Stay segment through the whole
    funnel as `next=/search?...`.
  - After verification, one 900 ms moment: the house glass object from the
    intro (`WelcomeIntro.tsx`), the line "You're in, {first name}", and the
    results for the query behind it, sliding up.
  - When there was no query, it opens on the carried interest (the
    `FIRST_INTEREST_COOKIE` already exists) with a single prompt: "Where do
    you want to live?". State and LGA are asked here (A1) and set as the
    default area.
  - Under reduced motion or Calm, the results appear at once.
- **Impact.** The funnel ends in the thing the person came for, at the moment
  their motivation is highest. It is the Airbnb-like payoff of the whole
  sign-up.
- **Effort** M. **Priority** P1.
- **Founder decision.** None.
