# Round 3: the sweep did not happen, and what each session does about it

**Session 1, 6 October 2026.** Four audits, run against the branches rather than
the response files. This replaces nothing in ROUND 2; it is the evidence behind it
and the work that follows.

---

## 1. Session 3: the foundation is excellent, the sweep is not done

**The one sentence: this is a very strong foundation release wearing a
platform-sweep label.**

What is genuinely built, and built well: the container tiers with a test,
`Odometer`, `HeroFigure`, `Segmented`, `Sheet`, `Toast`, the `Button` morph,
`PendingRing`, `DocumentSheet`, the navigation Island (301 new lines of
`nav-island.css` implementing 6.1 and 6.2 including the 44 to 100 pill widen and
the collapsed 72px desktop rail), the banned-phrase lint, 14 gallery pages, and the
glass `InnerNav` with real finger tracking, spring settle, haptics, keyboard,
Escape and a data-saver fallback.

**The startup sequence and Get Started are both built, and one decision in them is
better than the spec asked for:** `StartupSequence` draws from
`components/auth/vector-mark.ts` rather than the 614px PNG, because turning and
scaling a raster is exactly what goes soft on a 3x screen. Six beats, tap to skip,
4 second hard cap, reduced-motion crossfade. Feed, search and the tabs are real
work too: `SearchPillMorph` (189 new), `DiscoveryEmpty`, `ResultSkeleton`,
`PostCard +87`, `feed-m.css +245`.

**Now the measurements.**

North star section 13 required "each stage ends with the section 12 checklist run

`(app)/restaurants` is **19 lines**, `(app)/restaurant` 25, `(app)/stay` 92.

### The specific holes

1. **Two materials for the same legal text, in one release.** `(site)/terms`,
   `/privacy` and `/disclaimer` were migrated to `DocumentSheet`. Their in-app
   twins at `(app)/legal/*` still render the old `LegalDocument`. Six files, zero
   lines changed.
2. **`/payments`, the payer's own money screen: three lines**, one of them a
   comment. `/agent/earnings/page.tsx`: zero. `/settings/payments/page.tsx`: zero.
   Five settings pages untouched entirely.
3. **No receipts route, no member refunds route, no payouts screen** for a host or
   owner anywhere. The Paper register in D1 names thirteen surfaces; about half
   have no route to put paper on.
4. **The whole `(auth)` family is one mechanical edit**, `t={t}` to
   `t={forAuth(t)}`, across ten pages at three to five lines each. `auth.css`
   changed `+461/-750`, so material moved; page structure did not.
5. **`InnerNav` is mounted on 3 of 23 settings routes and on zero `agent` routes.**
   Only host got it. Its own docstring still names "the wallet (transactions,
   methods, statements, limits)" as a destination.
6. **Feature onboarding: 7 of 11 mounted.** Promotion and the Owner and Tenant
   command centre have no key and no route at all. Wallet, escrow and withdrawal
   exist with `body: null` and are the subject of D48. **And its persistence is a
   stub**: `serverFirstRunStore` returns `"unknown"` and writes nothing, so what
   ships is once per device by cookie. Clearing cookies re-shows every first run,
   which 14.1 explicitly forbids.
7. **Section 14.4, the premium artefact cards, do not exist.** No `ArtefactCard`,
   no fan, nothing. This is the one the founder pointed at directly ("image 5 ...
   premium glass card like cards debits cards"). Its four other uses, trust tiers,
   Pro plan selection, promotion tier selection and the badge-earned moment, have
   nothing either.
8. **Streaks are orphaned.** `StreakTile` is referenced only by the dev gallery.
   `EarnedMoment` has zero references anywhere. Section 15.1 is not in the product.
9. **`(app)/assistant`: zero lines.** `(app)/stories`: one line. `(app)/around`:
   32 lines across eight files. All three are named in D1's Night register.
10. **Host got a tenth of agent's budget.** `host` is 185 lines across 13 of 40
    files; `agent` is 1,933. Reference 7033, classified "canonical", is the host
    dashboard.

### What the audit cleared, and should be said

Raw colour: **no violation**, and `eslint-rules/no-raw-colour.mjs` enforces it. Em
dashes: **zero**. Banned phrases: systematised in `lib/copy/banned-phrases.ts`.
Glass navigation: not a violation, north star 6.1 requires it. **`Button` no longer
draws a spinner** (`PendingRing` replaced it), so Session 3's own Remaining is
stale on that point. One unbounded indicator remains, `animation.css:435`, and it
is reasoned in comment and collapses under reduced motion: a defensible deviation
rather than an oversight.

## 2. Session 2: three of eighteen sections done, and the two headline pieces are unwired

Done: the `/open` deadline, blocked terms, the four-digit passcode. Partial: the
Payluk findings, the provider seam, the rail router, ADR-0003 as a draft.

**Not started, eleven sections:** the append-only ledger and the nineteen financial
tables, the twelve escrow states and conditions engine, disputes, refunds,
cancellations and chargebacks, tax, webhooks and idempotency and reconciliation,
the referral engine, entitlements and paid promotion, the phone gate and vNIN and
Space Passport, standing streaks, the notification backbone and SMS, the event
layer, and the nine admin financial desks.

**The two pieces reported as built are scaffolding nothing calls.** Verified:
`lib/payments/router.ts` is referenced only by its own test. `lib/payments/provider.ts`
has no call site at all. The designs are good: dated policy that fails closed, a
trigger-frozen `transactions.rail`, its own probe. **No payment goes through either
of them.**

**R2-01, the wrong-payer refund, was not done.** `lib/payments/refund.ts` is
byte-identical to `main`. It was the one thing the brief said comes before
everything else.

**And one unasked change worth watching:** regenerating `database.types.ts`
(+14,525) dropped the enums, so `EscrowState` and `EscrowPurpose` are now
hand-written string unions in `lib/admin/reads/money-types.ts`. A type maintained by
hand against the database it mirrors will drift, and it will drift on money objects.

release lane, store submission, legal pages and the NDPC number, documentation,

high-severity advisory, and it found three gates that were measuring nothing and

## 5. Four agents each, and the rule for this round

Each session runs **four agents**, with declared file ownership and never two on one
file.

**Session 3:** C1 the sweep across untouched routes. C2 the money and settings
surfaces plus the three missing routes. C3 artefact cards, onboarding and streaks.
C4 D48, the auth family and the legal pages.

**Session 2:** B1 D40 and refunds and chargebacks. B2 wiring the rail router and
the provider seam, then the ledger. B3 tax, entitlements and promotion. B4 the
phone gate, referral qualification, the notification backbone and SMS.

**Session 4:** Q1 Task 2, the second audit. Q2 the device matrix and the native
lane. Q3 tests and gates. Q4 documents, store and operations.

**The rule: a number, not an adjective.** Every claim of progress in a response
file carries a count and a denominator. Routes audited of 213. Sections done of 18.
Matrix rows run of 34. "Upgraded", "swept" and "done" are not reportable without
one.
