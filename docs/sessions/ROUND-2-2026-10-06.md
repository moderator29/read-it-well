# Round 2: what is actually built, what is not, and the thirty recommendations

**Session 1, 6 October 2026.** The founder asked whether the three sessions'
"we are done" claims are true. **They are not, and each session's own response
file says so.** This document is the evidence, the gap list, and the work for
round two. It supersedes nothing: it adds.

---

## 1. The verdict, measured rather than asserted

Counted against `origin/main` on 6 October.

| Session | Commits | Files | Lines | Its own "Remaining" |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2, backend and money | 13 | 43 | +13,597 | **"Everything in handoff sections 7.1 to 7.18."** |
| 3, experience | 279 | 1,426 | +72,099 | 13 named items still in progress, plus 3 audits |
| 4, QA and release | 7 | 6 | +1,730 | 7 numbered items, the 34-row matrix first |

**Nobody lied about the work. The work is real and in several places it is
excellent.** Session 3's 279 commits are not a veneer: it shipped the component
library ports, the streak model with a test, 38 admin pages touched, the motion
work, and a measured framer-motion cost. Session 2 built the rail router as dated
policy that fails closed, a provider seam, and a kill switch. Session 4 found three
blind gates and closed them.

**What is untrue is "done".** A session that reports done while its own Remaining
section lists "everything in sections 7.1 to 7.18" has confused *this round is
finished* with *the brief is finished*. That is the thing to correct, and the
correction is cheap: the briefs below say what is left.

## 2. What is genuinely missing, verified by reading the tree

Checked on each branch rather than inferred.

### Not built at all

1. **Pro mode.** `pro_mode`, `proMode`, `isPro`: **zero occurrences on any
   branch.** The founder's instruction was explicit: a Pro switch, shown only to a
   member on a paid plan, and hidden entirely otherwise. There is no entitlement
   model, no plan record, no gate, no toggle.
2. **Paid promotion as a product.** 36 files mention promotion, but **no feature
   file exists**: nothing named promotion, boost or sponsor under `apps/web/src`.
   What exists is the V-06 constraint that forces `listings.featured = false` and
   the ranking test that asserts no paid input. The constraint is correct and
   stays until an ADR lifts it; the product it guards does not exist yet.
3. **Tax.** 27 files mention it, **no tax module**. D-level requirement, mechanism
   to be built regardless of the rate, which is counsel's.
4. **D40, the wrong-payer refund.** `refund.ts` on Session 2's branch has **zero**
   occurrences of `share_payer`. The defect I verified is untouched and it is
   still the most serious thing anyone has found.
5. **An admin referral desk.** The member side is complete and good
   (`settings/invite`, `referrals`, `referrals/[id]`, an honesty test). The admin
   side has `analytics` and `standing` but **no referral desk**: nowhere to see who
   referred whom, which referrals qualified, what was paid, or to reverse one.
6. **SMS beyond OTP.** `OtpTransport` exists. Transaction, booking, payout and
   dispute messages over SMS do not.
7. **The phone gate**, which the referral qualification depends on.
8. **Payluk anything.** Blocked on documentation the environment cannot reach.

### Built, but not finished to the brief

9. **Streaks**: model, tile and earned moment exist. No surface wires them to real
   on-time rent history, which is the whole point.
10. **The 24-point per-page audit**: Session 3 ran it on the surfaces it touched,
    and its Remaining names W12, the sweep at 390, 768 and 1440, as outstanding.
    Until W12 lands, "the whole platform is upgraded" is not a claim anyone can make.
11. **Spinners**: B5 reports `Button`'s non-morph `loading` still draws one. Vallo
    bans infinite spinners. Small, and it is exactly the kind of thing that makes
    the rest look unconsidered.

## 3. Thirty recommendations

Numbered R2-01 upward so a session can cite one. Each names its owner.

### Money, trust and correctness (Session 2)

- **R2-01. Fix D40 first, before anything else.** Nothing on the list matters if
  refunds can pay the wrong person.
- **R2-02. A refund dry-run.** Before any refund submits, show the desk the exact
  charge, payer, card tail and amount it is about to reverse, and require that to
  be acknowledged. D40 was possible because the desk could not see what it was
  about to do.
- **R2-03. Reconcile against the processor on a schedule**, not only on webhook.
  The one top-up ever recorded was credited 16 hours late by the reconciler rather
  than by the webhook, so the webhook has never been proven in production.
- **R2-04. A chargeback path.** Blind spot B-07. A dispute opened at the bank
  today has no route into Vallo at all.
- **R2-05. Make the Guarantee reserve auditable**: every contribution, every claim,
  every payout, as a ledger a person can read and total. The promise in
  `copy.ts` is specific and it should be checkable.
- **R2-06. Idempotency on every money write**, keyed on something the caller
  supplies. Payluk documents no idempotency key, which makes ours load-bearing.
- **R2-07. Tax as a mechanism with a null rate.** Build the withholding and the
  record now, leave the rate unset and refuse to compute rather than guess.
  Counsel supplies the number later and nothing else has to change.
- **R2-08. The phone gate**, then referral qualification behind it. The referral
  economics are worthless without it and it blocks a whole feature.
- **R2-09. SMS for the six moments that matter**: payment taken, payment failed,
  booking confirmed, inspection scheduled, agreement ready, payout settled. Over
  the existing Termii transport, DND route, with a per-member quiet-hours rule.
- **R2-10. An entitlement model** that Pro mode reads. One table, one server
  helper, no UI. Session 3 cannot build a Pro switch against nothing.
- **R2-11. Promotion as a schema that the ranking cannot see.** Paid placement
  lives in its own slot with its own label; `ranking.test.ts` keeps asserting no
  paid input. Build it so the constraint never has to be relaxed.
- **R2-12. An admin referral desk, server side**: who referred whom, qualification
  state, amount, and a reversal that is recorded rather than silent.

### Experience (Session 3)

- **R2-13. Finish W12 before claiming the sweep.** 390, 768 and 1440 on every
  route, with the 24-point audit recorded per page. The claim the founder cares
  about most is the one least evidenced.
- **R2-14. Kill the last spinner** (`Button` non-morph `loading`) and add a lint
  that fails on an infinite animation in a loading state, so it cannot return.
- **R2-15. Pro mode as a surface**, once R2-10 lands: hidden entirely when
  unentitled, never greyed out, never a teaser.
- **R2-16. The promotion surface**, once R2-11 lands, with the label legible at a
  glance and never dressed as an organic result.
- **R2-17. Wire streaks to real on-time rent history.** A streak computed from
  nothing is the anti-pattern the founder was warned about; computed from rent
  paid on time it is the most valuable thing on the platform.
- **R2-18. An empty state for every surface that can be empty**, drawn rather
  than blank. The platform has no inventory, so most members meet empty first.
- **R2-19. Error states with words and a next action**, never a bare toast.
- **R2-20. A reduced-motion pass over everything new.** The library's components
  vary: some honour it, some do not.
- **R2-21. One offline and one slow-network state.** Nigerian mobile data is the
  normal case, not the edge case.
- **R2-22. The 20 percent page-weight diet.** Session 4's budgets are the highest
  reading plus margin, deliberately loose. Page weight is the experience session's
  to bring down, and framer-motion just landed.
- **R2-23. A component gallery route** that renders every ported component in both
  themes, so drift is visible rather than discovered.
- **R2-24. Admin depth, not admin breadth.** 38 admin pages exist. Finish W8b and
  pick the five desks staff actually live in rather than polishing all 38 evenly.

### Quality, release and the system (Session 4)

- **R2-25. Run the 34-row matrix.** Ten are P0, zero have been run, and no session
  can do it from a container. This remains the single highest-value action.
- **R2-26. Correct the four wrong documents** it already identified. Cheap, and it
  stops the founder redoing work that is finished.
- **R2-27. A test that the root layout mounts `NativeRuntime`.** The outage that
  broke the app for every tester, with 8,791 tests passing, still has no
  regression test.
- **R2-28. Gate `scripts/marketing`.** It landed on main in PR 82 with its own
  lockfile and nothing audits it.
- **R2-29. Signed-in coverage.** The `QA_MEMBER_*` secrets unlock seven null
  weight budgets and signed-in accessibility. A settings change, not engineering.
- **R2-30. Fix the weight measurement variance** (`waitUntil: 'networkidle'`),
  after which every budget can come down and the gate gets sharper.

## 4. The standing rule this round adds

**"Done" means the brief, not the session.** A session reports *what it finished*
and *what remains*, in those words, and never reports completion while its own
Remaining section is non-empty. Where a session believes the brief is finished, it
says so against the brief's own acceptance criteria, one by one, with evidence.
